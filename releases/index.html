Version 17 BETA 1BETA

2026-10-28

Not tested yet. Download the .zip, or use Check for updates now in the app. To go back, use Check for updates now, then Go back to Version 16.

Fixed

  • Touch ring: going back to Version 15, changing a ring setting there and updating again turned every ring mode backwards. The app now puts the ring settings in the layout Version 15 reads before it installs Version 15 or older.
  • Touch ring: wheel thresholds go to the table on screen (All, or the selected app).

Changed

  • Photoshop: the Smooth zoom tip and the manual say what to do when the ring moves the canvas up and down instead of zooming: untick Zoom with Scroll Wheel in Photoshop’s Settings › Tools, or use Keystroke… with ⌘= / ⌘−.

Version 16Latest

2026-10-27

Added

  • Pen scroll (contributed by ftrabbold): hold a pen button or an ExpressKey and move the pen to scroll, like Wacom’s Pan/Scroll. Find it in the button editor under Scroll › Move pen to scroll.
  • Better support for Wacom tablets with limited support, not tested on these tablets yet: the Intuos Pro (PTH-460, PTH-660, PTH-860) ring turns the right way; touch strips on Intuos3 and on the Cintiq 21UX, 12WX and 22HD; the Cintiq 13HD centre button as ExpressKey 9; the Bamboo Fun ring; the Wacom CTK-4120 is newly listed.
  • Affinity: smooth zoom (Option + scroll wheel) and smooth rotate (⌘ + scroll wheel). For rotate, turn on Enable canvas rotation with ⌘+scroll wheel in Affinity’s Settings › Tools.

Fixed

  • The touch ring turns the right way: before, a clockwise turn zoomed out, made the brush smaller and rotated the canvas the wrong way on most Wacom rings. Your ring modes are turned the right way round when the app starts; click Save once to keep it.
  • With an app selected in the Application row, the ring’s On ticks, Function menus and Speed sliders no longer snap back.
  • Speed works everywhere: smooth zoom is much slower at low Speeds, and Speed 1 is possible.

Changed

  • The Supported tablets list leaves out notes for other operating systems and shows the new status Questionable.
  • The update window is translated in all 8 languages.
  • Tested as Version 16 BETA 2 before release. The user manual is updated and attached to the release as a PDF.
