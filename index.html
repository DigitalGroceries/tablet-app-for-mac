Tablet App for Mac

Free settings for your drawing tablet on a Mac with Apple silicon.

⬇ Download for Mac Version 6 · free
  1. Open the downloaded file, Tablet-App-for-Mac.dmg.
  2. Drag Tablet App for Mac onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open the app from Applications. If your Mac warns you, right-click the app and choose Open. If there is no Open button, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

When the app asks, allow Input Monitoring and Accessibility so it can read your pen. Quit the Wacom driver first. Need help? See the user manual.

Set up your pen and tablet

Pen pressure, buttons, the touch ring and ExpressKeys, laid out like the Wacom settings.

Apple silicon, macOS 27 · What is new
  • Free and open source (GPL-3.0)
  • No tracking or telemetry
  • Apple silicon
  • Tested on macOS 27.0.1 with an Intuos4 Small (PTK-440)

Tested only on that system and tablet. Use at your own risk.

What is new in Version 6

  • Touch ring: the dot sits under your finger; 12 o'clock is 0°, and clockwise reads Clockwise.
  • Pen button test: a held button lights on the pen picture.
  • Manual Pen Tip Pressure and Manual Eraser Pressure sliders, used while pressure sensitivity is off.
  • Starts silently at login, in the menu bar only. Only one copy runs.
  • Quit asks to save unsaved changes: Cancel, Don't Save or Save.

Support

Free to use. If it helps you, you can buy me a coffee with PayPal, Bitcoin or Monero. Any amount.

Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple silicon.
  • The OpenTabletDriver daemon (included).
  • Input Monitoring and Accessibility permissions.
  • Quit the Wacom driver first; both cannot run at once.
