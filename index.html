Your Wacom tablet, set up the Wacom way.

A free alternative to the Wacom driver for Apple silicon Macs. Wacom’s last Intuos driver only supports up to macOS 11. The Tablet App for Mac takes over from there, and brings back to life your trusted Wacom tablets.

Newer Wacom tablet? You can use this app instead of Wacom’s driver too. It already knows most current models. We never drop a tablet because it’s old, and if something breaks, we’ll do our best to fix it. We value your hard-earned money and stand against planned obsolescence.

The Tablet App for Mac is laid out like the original Wacom app interface, so there is nothing new to learn. Tested with a Wacom Intuos4 on macOS 27 Golden Gate on an Apple silicon Mac.

ATTENTION: TO INSTALL IT, FOLLOW THESE STEPS

The two permissions (Input Monitoring and Accessibility) are needed only at the first installation; the app guides you. From Version 18 on, updates keep them with nothing to do.

Updating from Version 17 or older: once, in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Input Monitoring and Device Control and Data Access (called Accessibility on older macOS), remove the old Tablet App for Mac entries with −, add the app with + and switch it on.

If the Wacom driver is installed, remove it first with Wacom's own uninstaller (Applications › Wacom Tablet › Wacom Tablet Utility), then restart your Mac.

  1. Open the downloaded file (Tablet-App-for-Mac.dmg), and drag Tablet App for Mac onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open the app from Applications. If Not Opened appears, click Done, not Move to Bin.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. macOS asks once more. Click Open Anyway, not the blue Move to Bin button.
  5. Confirm with Touch ID, or click Use Password… and enter your Mac login password.
  6. In System Settings › Privacy & Security › Input Monitoring, switch on Tablet App for Mac. This lets the app read your pen.
  7. In System Settings › Privacy & Security › Device Control and Data Access (called Accessibility on older macOS), switch on Tablet App for Mac. This lets the pen move the pointer.
  8. Quit the app and open it again.

Tip: if the pen still doesn't move the pointer, select Tablet App for Mac in those two lists, remove it with −, add it again with +, then reopen the app. Need more help? See the user manual.

What is new in Version 30

  • Keys that do nothing yet are easy to find. Home shows a big button, for example 6 ExpressKeys not set up: Click this button to choose what they do or restore to factory settings. On the ExpressKeys page those keys are marked Not set up, and when you press one on the tablet its row shows You just used this. It does nothing yet.
  • Restore factory settings sets up the ExpressKeys with Undo, Redo, B, I, V and Space (held), so a new tablet is ready to draw.
  • Radial menu and colour palette switches. On Tablet › Pen each has an Off / On switch. On asks which pen button opens it; Off gives that button back what it did before. The pictures are never greyed out.
  • Choose the palette’s colours in a window. Click the colour palette picture on Tablet › Pen: the palette opens large. Click a colour to pick your own (colour wheel, ready-made colours or a hex code); Default colours puts the original colours back.
  • One button design everywhere. Every button and tab has the same raised look, the chosen tab is lit, and CONNECTED on Home is green. The Movement and Pen input box is easy to read in the light looks.
  • A simpler Report saved window. Three numbered steps, a file you can drag onto the upload page, and if the page doesn’t open, the address to email the file to, with Copy address.
  • Help that takes you there. The manual’s Something doesn’t work? answers have buttons that open the right page in the app.
  • Two new languages: Hindi and Swahili. Choose one in Settings › System › Language. The app now speaks 13 languages.
  • Touch Ring centre button. A ring with nothing set on it gets the four factory modes, and its centre button switches them again.
  • Messages are easier to see. Confirmations such as Settings saved stay about 4.5 seconds.
  • Forget this tablet is now a red button on Home, instead of a ••• menu.
  • The Application row always shows which application you are setting up. Coming back to the Tablet page could light All while the page showed another application’s settings.
  • The Scribble page no longer shows the Pressure curve and smoothing tags.

Version 29

  • Colour palette on the pen. Press the pen button that opens the radial menu twice quickly: a colour wheel opens where the pen is, with 12 colours in light, muted and dark shades and a ring of greys. Tap a colour: in Photoshop it becomes the brush colour at once, and in every app its colour code is copied so you can paste it. The pencil in the middle changes a colour to your own.
  • Floating keyboard. Hold pen button 1 for about half a second and a small keyboard opens under the pen, so you can type with the pen, for example a layer name or a number. Its keys follow your Mac’s keyboard layout. A quick press still right-clicks. Its size and transparency are on the new Keyboard tab.
  • The radial menu stays open until you tap its ×, press the button again or press Esc, so you can tap several slices one after another (for example Undo three times). The radial menu and the colour palette can be open at the same time.
  • One radial menu and one colour palette for every application. Whichever app is in front, the pen opens the same menu and palette, and a change shows everywhere at once. Each app can still give the pen buttons other jobs.
  • A new Pen page. Tip Feel and Eraser Feel choose which end of the pen you set up, and the eraser now has its own pressure curve. The separate Eraser tab is gone. When you update, the eraser starts with the curve your tip had, so nothing feels different until you change it.
  • Type an exact value in the box next to any slider instead of dragging.
  • Brightness down and up as a button function (Media): they work like the Mac’s own brightness keys.
  • Touch Ring: one switch, one menu. Every ring has up to four modes; the factory ones are Rotate, Zoom, Brush size and Brush hardness. A ring you set up before keeps doing what it did.
  • Move the app’s window with the pen. Dragging the window by its top bar works with the pen as well as the mouse, also on macOS 27.2.
  • Setting search on Home. Type what you are looking for in the box under the tabs: the app lists the matching settings and where they are, in the app’s language. Click one and its page opens, with the setting framed for a moment.
  • Pen taps bring other apps forward without waiting. A pen tap on another app’s window no longer waits for the app’s window helper, which on some Macs made that window come forward up to 1 second late. If on your Mac a pen tap doesn’t switch to the other app, turn on App window switching fix on Advanced › Driver.
  • The eraser in GIMP. Turning the pen over now tells macOS that the pen tip has left before the eraser arrives, as a Wacom pen does, so apps that give the eraser its own tool (GIMP, for example) can switch to it. We couldn’t test this in GIMP ourselves, so please tell us how it works.
  • Touch speed and scroll momentum for finger touch (Intuos Pro PTH-651, Bamboo Pen & Touch): on Tablet › More › About, under Touch trackpad. Touch speed sets how far the pointer goes when one finger moves (50 is the old speed). Scroll momentum (off at first) lets two-finger scrolling glide on and slow to a stop, like a Mac trackpad.
  • Reports with a way to reply. If the email box is empty when you press Send with file, the report asks first: Add my email or Send without.
  • Three new languages: Italian, Swedish and Brazilian Portuguese. Choose one in Settings › System › Language.
  • Bluetooth tablets next to other Bluetooth devices. A tablet connected over Bluetooth is now found even when a Magic Trackpad or a Bluetooth mouse or keyboard is connected too. Please tell us how it works.
  • Tablets set up before they were connected. The app no longer crashes or keeps showing "Session data load failed" for such a tablet.
  • Smoother pen over Bluetooth. On a Wacom Intuos tablet connected over Bluetooth (CTL-4100WL), the app now uses every pen position the tablet sends, not one in four. Please tell us how it works.
  • A quiet start at login. When the app starts at login it shows no window and no "Checked for updates" message; only the pen icon in the menu bar shows.
  • Translations: the new texts are in all 11 languages, and choosing another language changes every text at once, without a restart.

Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple silicon.
  • macOS 15 or later.
  • The tablet driver, built on OpenTabletDriver (included).
  • Input Monitoring and Accessibility permissions (Accessibility is called Device Control and Data Access on macOS 27).
  • Uninstall the Wacom driver first, as both can't run at once.
